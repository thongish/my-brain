the cons are that there is limited customization compared to a custom-built system

it's pros are that there is minimal upfront cost and can be quickly implemented

its cons are that this strategy is even less customizable than the buy strategy
the long-term costs can exceed the initial savings
it is heavily dependent on vendor uptime and support
and there is no custody of data provided to the vendor
